2. Sensitive information and unique identifiers

Legal matters may involve sensitive information or unique identification information. K LAW OFFICE processes such information only where a separate consent or a specific legal basis is available as required by PIPA Articles 23 and 24 and related laws, and only to the extent necessary for the relevant legal matter.

3. Disclosure to third parties

K LAW OFFICE does not sell personal information and does not routinely disclose it outside the purposes for which it was processed. Information may be provided where the individual has consented, where disclosure is required or permitted by law, or where submission to a court, investigative authority, administrative body, counterparty, expert, or other relevant person or institution is reasonably necessary to perform legal services.

6. Destruction of personal information

When personal information is no longer necessary for its processing purpose and no lawful retention ground remains, K LAW OFFICE destroys it without undue delay. Electronic records are deleted using appropriate methods, and paper records are shredded or otherwise securely destroyed.

7. Cookies and automatically processed information

K LAW OFFICE does not use cookies for behavioral advertising or cross-site tracking on this website. Hosting or security providers may process technical information or use cookies necessary for website delivery, security, traffic management, or protection against malicious activity, depending on enabled service features.

8. Rights of individuals

Subject to applicable law, an individual may request access to, correction or deletion of, or suspension of processing of personal information. Requests may be submitted using the contact information below. Certain requests may be limited where retention or processing is required or permitted by law or necessary in connection with legal claims.

9. Security measures

K LAW OFFICE takes technical and organizational measures appropriate to the nature and scale of the information processed, including access control, account and authentication management, and use of security-enabled communications and cloud services.
